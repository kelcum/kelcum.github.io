Discord

Deadbolt

The Discord mod that survives Discord updates.

  • The whole Equicord library, 300+ plugins from Equicord and Vencord
  • Survives Discord updates: the installer puts Deadbolt back each time you sign in to Windows
  • Crash safe mode starts with core plugins only after two failed starts and names the suspect
  • Backup and restore your settings, QuickCSS and plugins from /deadbolt
  • Design your own badge, or a matching pair, with images and GIFs up to 5 MB
  • /addplugin and /myplugins: add, edit and share your own plugins without leaving Discord

Minecraft

Vitra

A Minecraft launcher made of glass, built for friends.

  • Mods and modpacks from Modrinth and CurseForge, installed in a click
  • Hypixel and the big servers one click away, with live player counts
  • Free capes, animated ones too, or turn any GIF into one
  • Any player's skin, searchable and wearable, plus your OptiFine cape

Roblox

Vesper

A Roblox launcher, tuned and verified.

  • Fast Flags Roblox still accepts, explained and checked against Roblox's own log after every launch
  • Your picture as the emote wheel, cut into a circle with Roblox's slots on top
  • Cursors in any colour and size, Shift Lock included, plus your own font, death sound and sky
  • Lowest-ping servers, or one in the region you pick
  • Shaders and older Roblox versions on Windows, kept apart from your normal install
  • Discord Rich Presence with your game and a Join button, off until you turn it on
